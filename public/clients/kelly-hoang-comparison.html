What this means for your target

Of the four scenarios above, the $300,000 primary residence purchase at 20 percent down is the only one that lands at or under your $2,000 monthly target. Adding 15 percent down financing or purchasing at $325,000 both push the payment above that mark, while the investment property scenario carries the highest rate of the group and the highest cash needed at closing.
