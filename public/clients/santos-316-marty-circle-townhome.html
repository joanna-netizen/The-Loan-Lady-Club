HOA dues are paid to the association, not the lender, but they count as part of the monthly housing payment. It is worth confirming what the dues cover, since some associations include exterior maintenance, water, or a master insurance policy, which can change the insurance quote. How to read these: a closing cost credit lowers what Angelica brings to the table. A 2-1 buy-down lowers the payment for the first two years only, and she still has to qualify at the full note rate. The HOA dues stay the same through all three years, so the buy-down only affects the mortgage portion. A permanent buy-down lowers the rate for the life of the loan, and how much one more point moves the rate depends on pricing the day it is run. A seller credit can be split between closing costs and a buy-down, as long as the total stays within the 3% limit and does not exceed actual closing costs.
