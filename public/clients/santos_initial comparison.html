How to read these: a closing cost credit lowers what Angelica brings to the table. A 2-1 buy-down lowers the payment for the first two years only, and she still has to qualify at the full note rate. A permanent buy-down lowers the rate for the life of the loan, and how much one more point moves the rate depends on pricing the day it is run. A seller credit can also be split, part toward closing costs and part toward a buy-down, as long as the total stays within the 3% limit and does not exceed actual closing costs.
